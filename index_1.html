FAQ

Features that feel personal,
yet powerfully scalable

Everything you need to know about working with Global Quest Co. — from orders to delivery.

What is the minimum order quantity?

+

For uniforms, our minimum order is 50 units per style/design. For hygiene products and corporate gifts, quantities vary by product — contact us for specific details.

How long does production take?

+

Standard production takes 2–3 weeks after design approval. Rush orders are available — speak to our team for express timelines.

Do you offer logo embroidery and printing?

+

Yes! We offer logo embroidery, screen printing, heat transfer, and digital printing on all uniform types. Our design team can help create or refine your artwork.

Which countries do you deliver to?

+

We ship across the entire GCC region (Saudi Arabia, UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain, Oman) and multiple African countries. Contact us to confirm availability for your location.

Can I get samples before bulk order?

+

Absolutely. We provide sample garments for quality approval before bulk production begins. Sample lead time is typically 5–7 business days.
